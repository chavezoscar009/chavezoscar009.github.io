<h3>Playing guitar</h3>
<p>I play guitar and I'm passionate about rock, blues rock, and soul rock. Music is a big part of my life and a steady source of inspiration — I picked up the guitar in 2020, and it's been a constant source of joy ever since to express myself creatively through the instrument. I hope to play like some of my idols someday — Slash, Synyster Gates, Marcus King, Derek Trucks, Eric Clapton — but until then, happy to keep getting better little by little, making music that soothes me and hopefully other people too.</p>
<p>You can check out some of my playing on <a href="https://soundcloud.com/chavezoscar009" target="_blank" rel="noopener">SoundCloud</a> or see live performances on my <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@chavezoscar009/videos" target="_blank" rel="noopener">YouTube channel</a>.</p>

<h3>Playing soccer &amp; fitness</h3>
<p>I enjoy playing soccer whenever I'm healthy and able to — I've been playing since I was 7. It's technically challenging but also genuinely beautiful to watch when you see great ball control, individual skill, and teamwork; I suppose that's why they call it the beautiful game. I love the challenge and camaraderie that comes from playing on a team. On weekends you can probably find me on the pitch with my team, HeartBeat City FC, and I'll dabble in pick-up soccer too, mostly just to have fun and keep the touch up.</p>

<h3>Playing board &amp; card games</h3>
<p>I love hosting game nights and playing board and card games with friends — one of my favorite ways to unwind and connect with people. There's something about a good game that brings people together, and the strategy involved in winning always fascinates me.</p>
